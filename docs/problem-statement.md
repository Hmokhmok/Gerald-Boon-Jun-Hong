# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
E-commerce sites and Ecommerce users buying shoes.

## What exactly is going wrong?
Shoe e-commerce space return rates are an issue and this is due to the users having low purchasing confidance as well as buying the wrong shoe sizes.

## Why does it matter?
When the user is not satisfied with their new shoe they could possibly return it which is a cost that e-commerce company have to bear

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)

Shopping for shoes is really convenient but most customers suffer from the same problem which is the inability to try on those shiny new footwear. Even though the ability to return shoes is a good feature for online shopping platforms, this outcome is preferably an outcome companies would avoid to reduce cost and maintain customer satisfaction from their initial purchase. Furthermore, inconsistent shoe sizes and users lack of measuring equipment contribute to lower purchasing confidence. Online shopping through AR can allow customer to  convenient view products in same condition as they will be used, even as they stay safe at home (Karkour et al., 2022). Augmented reality allows users to have a better idea of the shoes on their own feet in real time through their mobile device, giving them a better understanding of the proportion and appearance of the shoe before the purchase.
